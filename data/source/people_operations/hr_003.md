# Employee Onboarding Process

## Purpose and Scope
This document specifies standard onboarding workflows, coordination checkpoints, and resource readiness milestones for incoming employees at Aster Systems. It applies to hiring managers, human resources specialists, and support teams welcoming regular staff and contract personnel. Structured onboarding ensures seamless welcoming experiences and operational readiness for new team members.

## Onboarding Preparation
Coordinated advance planning across departmental stakeholders ensures smooth first-day experiences and operational readiness for incoming personnel.
<!-- fact_id: HR003_F01 -->
People operations coordinators must finalize and circulate the comprehensive new-hire checklist at least 5 business days before the joining date to enable timely equipment preparation and workspace allocation. Completing preparations in advance ensures administrative readiness before the new hire arrives.

## Account Provisioning
Corporate identity accounts, digital credentials, and directory memberships must be configured systematically to prevent first-day operational delays.
<!-- fact_id: HR003_F02 -->
All new hire system access setups and account creation workflows are executed in coordination with technical IT procedures established under ITS-002. Automated identity configurations ensure necessary collaboration spaces and tools are accessible upon arrival.

## Laptop Provisioning
Computing hardware and peripheral equipment must be pre-configured, tested, and assigned before the employee begins work.
<!-- fact_id: HR003_F03 -->
Hardware procurement, standard device configuration, and physical handover protocols follow the equipment provisioning framework specified in ITS-001. Standardized hardware bundles ensure incoming personnel have reliable workstations on their first day.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
User account setup, identity group assignments, and access validation for incoming personnel connect directly to separate technical account provisioning workflows. Departmental orientation and team introductions are coordinated directly within individual business units.

Version v1 is effective from 2026-11-01. This initial process formalizes new-hire onboarding checklists and cross-functional coordination standards.
