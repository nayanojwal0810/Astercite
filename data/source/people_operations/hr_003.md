# Employee Onboarding Process

## Purpose and Scope
This document specifies standard onboarding workflows, cross-departmental coordination checkpoints, and resource readiness milestones for incoming employees at Aster Systems. It applies to hiring managers, human resources specialists, and support teams welcoming regular staff and contract personnel.

## Onboarding Preparation
Coordinated advance planning across departmental stakeholders ensures seamless first-day experiences and operational readiness for new team members.
<!-- fact_id: HR003_F01 -->
People operations coordinators must finalize and circulate the comprehensive new-hire checklist at least 5 business_days_before_join to enable timely equipment preparation, workspace allocation, and account scheduling.

## Account Provisioning
Corporate identity accounts, digital credentials, and directory group memberships must be configured systematically to prevent first-day operational blockers.
<!-- fact_id: HR003_F02 -->
All new hire system access setups and account creation workflows are executed in coordination with technical IT procedures established under ITS-002. Automated identity synchronizations ensure necessary team repositories and collaboration spaces are accessible upon arrival.

## Laptop Provisioning
Computing hardware and peripheral equipment must be pre-configured, tested, and assigned before the employee begins their initial onboarding orientation.
<!-- fact_id: HR003_F03 -->
Hardware procurement, standard device configuration, and physical handover protocols follow the equipment provisioning framework specified in ITS-001. Workstation bundles include standardized security baselines and pre-installed core productivity software.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
User account setup, identity group assignments, and access validation for incoming personnel depend directly on the technical execution workflows detailed in ITS-002. Departmental training and mentor pairings are managed independently within individual business divisions.
