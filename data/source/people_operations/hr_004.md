# Learning and Training Reimbursement

## Purpose and Scope
This policy defines eligibility, funding limits, approval requirements, and reimbursement workflows for professional development courses, certifications, and technical conferences. It applies to permanent employees across Aster Systems seeking to enhance relevant professional skills. Supporting continuing education encourages skill development and operational excellence across the organization.

## Training Reimbursement Limit
Aster Systems encourages professional development through financial subsidies for accredited courses, technical workshops, and professional credentials.
<!-- fact_id: HR004_F01 -->
Eligible full-time employees may receive financial support up to an annual limit of INR 20,000 per year toward approved educational programs. This annual allowance helps employees acquire relevant skills and professional certifications.

## Training Approval
Educational activities must align with the employee's current responsibilities or agreed departmental growth objectives.
<!-- fact_id: HR004_F02 -->
Prior to registering for any course, examination, or conference, the candidate must secure written approval from their immediate team lead. Obtaining pre-approval ensures the training program aligns with team goals and operational priorities.

## Reimbursement Claim Deadline
Timely financial reconciliation ensures accurate budget tracking and prompt reimbursement of approved course fees.
<!-- fact_id: HR004_F03 -->
Employees must submit completed reimbursement documentation along with proof of successful program completion within 30 calendar days after completion. Prompt claim filing ensures expenses are processed and reconciled in a timely manner.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
All financial disbursements and formal reimbursement filings for approved educational expenditures connect directly to the expense claim submission process set forth in OPS-004.

## Revision History
Version v1 is effective from 2026-11-01. This initial policy establishes annual learning allowances and pre-approval requirements.
