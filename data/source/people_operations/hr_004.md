# Learning and Training Reimbursement

## Purpose and Scope
This policy defines eligibility, funding limits, approval requirements, and reimbursement workflows for professional development courses, certifications, and technical conferences. It applies to all permanent employees across Aster Systems seeking to enhance relevant professional skills.

## Training Reimbursement Limit
Aster Systems encourages continuous career development through financial subsidies for accredited courses, technical workshops, and professional credentials.
<!-- fact_id: HR004_F01 -->
Eligible full-time employees may receive financial support up to a maximum annual cap of 20000 inr per fiscal calendar year toward approved educational programs. Unused annual training balances do not roll over to subsequent fiscal years.

## Training Approval
Educational activities must directly correlate with the employee's current responsibilities or agreed departmental growth trajectories.
<!-- fact_id: HR004_F02 -->
Prior to registering for any course, examination, or conference, the candidate must secure written sponsorship from their immediate team_lead confirming operational relevance and schedule feasibility. Registrations completed without prior sign-off are ineligible for reimbursement.

## Reimbursement Claim Deadline
Prompt financial reconciliation ensures accurate departmental budget tracking and timely disbursement of approved course fees.
<!-- fact_id: HR004_F03 -->
Employees must submit completed reimbursement documentation along with proof of successful course completion or certification within 30 calendar_days following program conclusion. Submissions received beyond this window are subject to administrative forfeiture.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
All financial disbursements and formal reimbursement filings for approved educational expenditures are processed in accordance with the general claim submission procedures set forth in OPS-004.
