# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Purpose and Scope
This policy defines operational standards, expectations, and administrative procedures governing hybrid scheduling and remote work arrangements at Aster Systems. It applies to all eligible employees whose job roles permit distributed work execution while maintaining cross-functional team collaboration.

## Office Attendance Requirements
Aster Systems maintains a hybrid operating model designed to balance flexible working practices with necessary face-to-face team alignment and collaboration.
<!-- fact_id: HR002_F01 -->
Under standard hybrid working arrangements, all regular employees are required to fulfill a minimum office attendance of 2 days_per_week at their designated regional Aster Systems facility. Specific onsite scheduling days are coordinated within individual department units.

## Remote Work Exceptions
Special personal circumstances, health accommodations, or temporary travel may warrant adjustments to the standard hybrid working pattern.
<!-- fact_id: HR002_F02 -->
Any temporary or long-term exception to mandatory office attendance schedules must receive written evaluation and authorization from the employee's assigned team_lead before modified arrangements take effect. Formal exception records are filed with people operations.

## Remote Work Equipment
Employees working from remote workstations are equipped with standardized computing assets and approved tools to perform their responsibilities safely and efficiently.
<!-- fact_id: HR002_F03 -->
Hardware assets, computing peripherals, and digital software suites required for remote work are governed under the equipment protocols established in ITS-004_and_OPS-005. Personal consumer hardware must not connect directly to sensitive production networks.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
Application software requested by remote personnel for distributed computing tasks must be submitted through the approval process defined in ITS-004. Corporate hardware allocation and return procedures remain aligned with standard asset management policies.
