# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Purpose and Scope
This policy defines operational standards, expectations, and administrative procedures governing hybrid scheduling and remote work arrangements at Aster Systems. It applies to eligible employees whose job roles permit distributed work execution while maintaining cross-functional team collaboration. Clear hybrid standards balance flexible working practices with necessary in-person team coordination.

## Office Attendance Requirements
Aster Systems maintains a hybrid operating model designed to balance flexible working practices with in-person team alignment and collaboration.
<!-- fact_id: HR002_F01 -->
Under standard hybrid working arrangements, regular employees are required to fulfill a minimum office attendance of 2 days per week at their designated regional facility. Regular onsite presence fosters collaboration and strengthens cross-functional relationships.

## Remote Work Exceptions
Special personal circumstances, health accommodations, or temporary operational requirements may warrant adjustments to standard hybrid schedules.
<!-- fact_id: HR002_F02 -->
Any exception to mandatory office attendance schedules must receive approval from the employee's assigned team lead before modified arrangements take effect. Review by the team lead ensures operational coverage remains intact during modified work schedules.

## Remote Work Equipment
Employees working from remote workstations are equipped with standardized computing assets and approved tools to perform their responsibilities safely and efficiently.
<!-- fact_id: HR002_F03 -->
Hardware assets, computing peripherals, and digital software suites required for remote work are governed under the equipment protocols established in ITS-004 and OPS-005. Standardized tools ensure consistent computing capabilities across distributed working environments.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
Application software requested by remote personnel for distributed computing tasks must be submitted through the approval process defined in ITS-004. Corporate hardware allocation and return procedures remain aligned with standard asset management policies.
