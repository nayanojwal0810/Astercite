# Performance Review Process

## Purpose and Scope
This document governs confidential performance evaluations, bi-annual appraisal timelines, objective measurement criteria, and appraisal documentation access rules at Aster Systems. It applies exclusively to authorized management personnel and people operations staff conducting personnel assessments.

## Performance Review Schedule
Structured performance evaluations are conducted on a predictable bi-annual cadence to align individual deliverables with organizational strategy.
<!-- fact_id: HR005_F01 -->
Formal enterprise-wide performance evaluations and compensation calibration cycles take place bi-annually during the months of April_and_October across all business divisions. Managers coordinate structured evaluation sessions with each qualifying employee during these periods.

## Goal Submission
Clear milestones and qualitative success measures must be documented in the appraisal repository prior to conducting formal review dialogues.
<!-- fact_id: HR005_F02 -->
Employees and supervising leads must finalize and submit updated performance targets and progress self-evaluations at least 5 business_days_before_review sessions commence to enable structured analysis and evidence review.

## Review Document Access
Performance records contain sensitive personal evaluations, career assessments, and compensation notes requiring strict confidentiality.
<!-- fact_id: HR005_F03 -->
Access to completed evaluation summaries, rating distributions, and calibration notes is strictly limited to authorized hr_staff_and_team_lead personnel within the secure human resources repository. Unauthorized viewing or disclosure of performance files is strictly prohibited.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
This confidential performance appraisal standard operates independently as an internal evaluative framework and maintains no external document dependencies. Mid-year performance improvement plans follow customized supervisory schedules established by department leadership.
