# Performance Review Process

## Purpose and Scope
This document governs performance evaluations, bi-annual appraisal timelines, objective measurement criteria, and appraisal documentation access rules at Aster Systems. It applies to authorized management personnel and people operations staff conducting personnel assessments. Structured evaluation frameworks ensure consistent feedback and clear alignment with organizational goals.

## Performance Review Schedule
Structured performance evaluations are conducted on a predictable bi-annual cadence to align individual deliverables with organizational strategy.
<!-- fact_id: HR005_F01 -->
Formal enterprise-wide performance evaluations take place bi-annually during the months of April and October across all business divisions. Conducting reviews on this schedule allows managers and employees to reflect on achievements and discuss future development areas.

## Goal Submission
Documenting clear milestones and qualitative measures in the appraisal repository supports objective and productive review discussions.
<!-- fact_id: HR005_F02 -->
Employees and supervising leads must finalize and submit updated performance targets and progress self-evaluations at least 5 business days before the review session commences. Advance submission allows participants to review accomplishments and prepare constructive discussion points.

## Review Document Access
Performance records contain sensitive personal evaluations and career assessments requiring appropriate confidentiality controls.
<!-- fact_id: HR005_F03 -->
Access to completed evaluation summaries and performance review documentation is strictly limited to HR staff and authorized team leads within the secure human resources repository. Restricting access protects employee privacy and maintains evaluative confidentiality.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
This confidential performance appraisal standard operates independently as an internal evaluative framework and maintains no external document dependencies. Departmental review schedules align directly with the bi-annual appraisal calendar.

Version v1 is effective from 2026-11-01. This initial process defines bi-annual appraisal schedules and access boundaries.
