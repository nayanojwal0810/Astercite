# Grievance and Escalation Procedure

## Purpose and Scope
This procedure provides a clear, fair, and confidential framework for reporting, investigating, and resolving workplace concerns, ethical complaints, and interpersonal conflicts at Aster Systems. It is available to employees across the organization. Structured escalation mechanisms ensure issues are handled impartially and in accordance with established procedural guidelines.

## Grievance Acknowledgment
Prompt receipt confirmation reassures initiating individuals that their concerns have been formally received and queued for review.
<!-- fact_id: HR006_F01 -->
Upon formal submission of an issue via confidential intake channels, people operations officers must provide an official receipt acknowledgment within 2 business days. Timely acknowledgment confirms receipt and initiates preliminary case intake.

## Grievance Appeals
When an employee seeks secondary review of an initial investigation outcome, a structured appeal window is provided.
<!-- fact_id: HR006_F02 -->
A formal request for secondary review or decision appeal must be lodged within 10 business days following delivery of the preliminary investigation summary. Defining an appeal window ensures concerns are revisited in a timely and structured manner.

## Private Grievance Case Access
Protecting the privacy and confidentiality of individuals involved in workplace complaints is an essential operational imperative.
<!-- fact_id: HR006_F03 -->
Direct viewing permissions and investigative case files concerning confidential complaints are restricted strictly to HR staff only within the secure employee relations case registry. Limiting record access preserves confidentiality throughout the dispute resolution process.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
This dispute resolution framework functions independently as the primary workplace escalation mechanism across Aster Systems and maintains no external document dependencies. Case reviews follow standard internal procedures.

## Revision History
Version v1 is effective from 2026-11-01. This initial procedure establishes standard escalation protocols and appeal windows for workplace grievances.
