# Grievance and Escalation Procedure

## Purpose and Scope
This procedure provides a transparent, fair, and confidential framework for reporting, investigating, and resolving workplace concerns, ethical complaints, and interpersonal conflicts at Aster Systems. It is available to all employees, contractors, and interns without fear of retaliation.

## Grievance Acknowledgment
Prompt receipt confirmation reassures initiating individuals that their concerns are handled with seriousness and procedural urgency.
<!-- fact_id: HR006_F01 -->
Upon formal submission of an issue via confidential intake channels, people operations officers must provide an official receipt acknowledgment within 2 business_days to confirm receipt and assign an intake case identifier.

## Grievance Appeals
When an employee believes that an initial inquiry reached an unsatisfactory conclusion or omitted material evidence, an escalation avenue is provided.
<!-- fact_id: HR006_F02 -->
A formal request for secondary review or decision appeal must be lodged within 10 business_days following delivery of the preliminary investigation summary. Late appeal filings are accepted only under documented extenuating circumstances.

## Private Grievance Case Access
Protecting the privacy and confidentiality of individuals submitting sensitive workplace allegations is a primary legal and operational imperative.
<!-- fact_id: HR006_F03 -->
Direct viewing permissions and investigative case files concerning confidential complaints are restricted strictly to authorized hr_staff_only within the secure employee relations case registry. Unauthorized sharing of investigative records is grounds for disciplinary action.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
This dispute resolution framework functions independently as the primary workplace escalation mechanism across Aster Systems and maintains no external document dependencies. Allegations involving statutory regulatory matters may be referred to external legal counsel.
