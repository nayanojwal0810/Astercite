# Leave and Attendance Policy

## Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes foundational guidelines for statutory and discretionary employee leave, attendance tracking, and absence reporting across Aster Systems. It applies to all full-time and part-time personnel employed across corporate office facilities and authorized remote locations.

## Annual Leave Entitlement
Aster Systems provides annual paid vacation time to support employee well-being, work-life balance, and continuous personal rejuvenation.
<!-- fact_id: HR001_F01 -->
Full-time employees accrue an annual paid vacation entitlement of 18 days_per_year, credited proportionally across monthly payroll cycles. Unused days may be carried forward in accordance with company benefits schedules.

## Sick Leave Entitlement
Employees experiencing acute illness, medical appointments, or unanticipated health conditions are supported through paid sick leave allowances.
<!-- fact_id: HR001_F02 -->
All eligible employees receive an annual allocation of 10 days_per_year of paid sick leave, designed to accommodate short-term medical recovery without loss of basic compensation. Medical certification may be requested for consecutive absences exceeding three calendar days.

## Leave Request Process
Advance planning and clear communication ensure smooth project continuity and equitable workload distribution across teams during planned employee absences.
<!-- fact_id: HR001_F03 -->
Planned leave requests must be submitted through the human resources portal at least 3 business_days prior to the anticipated absence date to enable manager review and scheduling coordination. Urgent emergencies are reported directly to the immediate supervisor.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
This core attendance and leave framework operates independently across all personnel categories and maintains no external document dependencies. Statutory parental and bereavement leaves are administered in accordance with applicable regional labor statutes.
