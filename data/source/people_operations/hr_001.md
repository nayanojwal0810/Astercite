# Leave and Attendance Policy

## Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes foundational guidelines for employee leave, attendance tracking, and absence reporting across Aster Systems. It applies to employees across corporate office facilities and authorized remote locations. Structured leave policies support employee well-being while ensuring consistent operational planning across teams.

## Annual Leave Entitlement
Aster Systems provides annual vacation time to support employee rest, personal commitments, and work-life balance.
<!-- fact_id: HR001_F01 -->
Employees receive 18 days of annual leave per year. Planned vacation time allows personnel to rest and recharge while enabling teams to plan workload coverage accordingly.

## Sick Leave Entitlement
Employees experiencing acute illness, medical appointments, or unanticipated health conditions are supported through dedicated sick leave allowances.
<!-- fact_id: HR001_F02 -->
Employees receive 10 days of sick leave per year to accommodate medical recovery. Providing dedicated sick leave ensures employees can prioritize health and recuperation when unwell.

## Leave Request Process
Advance planning and clear communication ensure project continuity and balanced workload distribution across teams during planned employee absences.
<!-- fact_id: HR001_F03 -->
Planned leave requests must be submitted at least 3 business days before the anticipated absence date. Advance submission enables colleagues and teams to plan workloads and coordinate project responsibilities effectively.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
This core attendance and leave framework operates independently across all personnel categories and maintains no external document dependencies. Departmental scheduling aligns directly with standard leave notification procedures.
