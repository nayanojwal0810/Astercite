# Travel Reimbursement Policy

## Purpose and Scope
This policy defines allowable expenses, daily allowances, travel approval protocols, and reimbursement procedures for business trips undertaken on behalf of Aster Systems. It applies to regular personnel and managers traveling domestically or internationally on company business. Clear travel guidelines ensure predictable budget management and consistent reimbursement processing.

## Domestic Travel Allowance
To provide support for meals and incidental expenses during official domestic business travel, a standardized daily allowance is established.
<!-- fact_id: OPS001_F01 -->
Employees traveling on authorized domestic business itineraries are entitled to a standard per diem subsistence allowance of INR 1,500 per day to offset daily travel expenditures. The daily allowance provides predictable financial support while employees represent the company away from their home office.

## Travel Booking Approval
Reviewing travel plans prior to purchase ensures business necessity and maintains alignment with departmental operational budgets.
<!-- fact_id: OPS001_F02 -->
Formal managerial travel booking approval is required before booking airline tickets, hotel reservations, or intercity transportation. Securing advance sign-off confirms that proposed travel plans are authorized and aligned with project requirements.

## Travel Expense Claims
Following completion of authorized travel itineraries, employees reconcile incurred business travel expenditures through established accounting processes.
<!-- fact_id: OPS001_F03 -->
Reimbursement filings for incurred corporate travel expenses connect directly to the general expense claim procedures set forth in OPS-004. Submitting travel expense reports allows finance personnel to audit claims and disburse reimbursements systematically.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
All post-travel reimbursement requests, invoice validation routines, and payment disbursements connect directly to the claims submission process specified in OPS-004. Travel expense management operates in close coordination with central accounting guidelines.

## Revision History
Version v1 is effective from 2026-11-01. This initial policy establishes domestic per diem rates and pre-travel authorization controls.
