# Travel Reimbursement Policy

## Purpose and Scope
This policy defines allowable expenses, daily allowances, travel approval protocols, and reimbursement procedures for business trips undertaken on behalf of Aster Systems. It applies to all permanent staff, managers, and operational specialists traveling domestically or internationally.

## Domestic Travel Allowance
To provide predictable support for standard meals, local incidentals, and ground transport during official company travel, a standardized daily stipend is established.
<!-- fact_id: OPS001_F01 -->
Employees traveling on authorized domestic business itineraries are entitled to a standard per diem subsistence allowance of 1500 inr_per_day to offset meals and minor local travel costs without requiring itemized receipts for minor sustenance.

## Travel Booking Approval
Unplanned travel expenditures present budgetary risks and must be authorized prior to issuing commercial purchase orders or purchasing tickets.
<!-- fact_id: OPS001_F02 -->
Formal managerial travel booking approval is strictly required_before_booking airline tickets, hotel reservations, or intercity transportation to ensure operational necessity and budget availability. Unapproved reservations cannot be guaranteed for corporate reimbursement.

## Travel Expense Claims
Following completion of authorized travel itineraries, employees reconcile actual travel expenditures through centralized financial accounting channels.
<!-- fact_id: OPS001_F03 -->
Reimbursement filings for all incurred corporate travel expenses are governed under the general expense submission procedures set forth in OPS-004. Travelers must submit original tax invoices alongside their completed claims.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
All post-travel reimbursement requests, invoice validation routines, and payment disbursements depend directly on the claims submission process specified in OPS-004. Corporate client entertainment during business travel requires secondary departmental line-item authorization.
