# Purchase Approval Limits

## Purpose and Scope
This policy defines formal spending authority tiers, management escalation levels, and financial approvals required for corporate purchases and vendor contracts at Aster Systems. It applies to team leads and finance operations personnel responsible for evaluating and authorizing organizational expenditures. Tiered spending thresholds ensure appropriate fiscal oversight while enabling efficient operational purchasing.

## Team Lead Purchase Approval
Frontline operational leads are granted discretionary purchasing authority to acquire essential team supplies, developer tools, and routine materials.
<!-- fact_id: OPS002_F01 -->
An individual team lead is authorized to approve purchases and requisitions up to INR 25,000 alone without requiring escalation to central finance staff. Discretionary limits enable managers to resolve immediate operational requirements efficiently.

## Finance Purchase Approval
Expenditures exceeding frontline limits undergo budgetary assessment and accounting verification by dedicated finance personnel.
<!-- fact_id: OPS002_F02 -->
Requisitions falling within the expenditure range above INR 25,000 through INR 100,000 require independent financial assessment and formal authorization from Finance staff before purchase orders are issued. Finance review verifies budget availability and expenditure classification.

## High-Value Purchase Approval
Capital investments, multi-user software licenses, and substantial operational contracts require joint evaluation across technical and financial leadership.
<!-- fact_id: OPS002_F03 -->
All corporate procurement activities and capital purchases exceeding INR 100,000 require approval from both Finance staff and a team lead before commercial commitments are executed. Dual authorization ensures major financial outlays receive thorough operational and fiscal review.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
This baseline authorization policy establishes governing financial thresholds and maintains no outgoing document dependencies. It serves as an authoritative limit schedule receiving incoming equipment procurement requisitions under OPS-005 and elevated expense claims forwarded from OPS-004.
