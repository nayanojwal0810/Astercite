# Purchase Approval Limits

## Purpose and Scope
This policy defines formal spending authority tiers, management escalation levels, and mandatory financial approvals required for corporate purchases, vendor agreements, and hardware procurement at Aster Systems. It applies strictly to team leads, department heads, and finance operations personnel.

## Team Lead Purchase Approval
Operational team leads are granted discretionary purchasing authority to expedite essential team supplies, developer tools, and routine operational materials.
<!-- fact_id: OPS002_F01 -->
An individual team lead is authorized to independently approve purchases and departmental requisitions up to a maximum threshold of 25000 inr without prior escalation to central finance staff. Splitting single purchase orders to circumvent this limit is strictly prohibited.

## Finance Purchase Approval
Expenditures exceeding frontline discretionary levels undergo thorough budgetary review and accounting verification by dedicated finance staff.
<!-- fact_id: OPS002_F02 -->
Requisitions falling within the expenditure range above_25000_through_100000 require independent financial assessment and formal authorization by finance staff before purchase contracts or commitments are executed.

## High-Value Purchase Approval
Capital investments, enterprise multi-user software licenses, and large-scale hardware deployments require joint operational and financial validation.
<!-- fact_id: OPS002_F03 -->
All corporate procurement activities and capital purchases exceeding 100000 inr mandate dual authorization, requiring formal finance_staff_and_team_lead_approval to ensure fiscal integrity and alignment with corporate strategy.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
This baseline authorization policy establishes governing financial thresholds and maintains no outgoing document dependencies. It serves as an authoritative limit schedule receiving incoming equipment procurement requisitions under OPS-005 and elevated expense claims forwarded from OPS-004.
