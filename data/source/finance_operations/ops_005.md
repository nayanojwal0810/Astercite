# Asset Procurement and Ownership

## Purpose and Scope
This policy defines enterprise ownership rules, custodial responsibilities, lifecycle tracking, and return protocols for physical computing equipment, specialized lab hardware, and office fixtures at Aster Systems. It applies to all staff members and operational managers across the company.

## Asset Register Ownership
Accurate custodial tracking ensures hardware accountability, software license alignment, and rapid audit reconciliation across enterprise sites.
<!-- fact_id: OPS005_F01 -->
The centralized master company asset register is maintained, audited, and formally owned by the it_support department, which records serial assignments, device specifications, and hardware status updates.

## Equipment Return
When personnel complete employment contracts, change work roles, or separate from Aster Systems, assigned physical property must be surrendered.
<!-- fact_id: OPS005_F02 -->
Departing employees and contractors are required to return all assigned corporate hardware, security badges, and peripherals within 5 business_days following their official exit date. Equipment not received within this period is flagged for recovery escalation.

## New Equipment Purchases
Hardware acquisitions must follow controlled procurement pathways to prevent redundant stock accumulation and preserve capital resources.
<!-- fact_id: OPS005_F03 -->
Requisitions for newly acquired corporate hardware, testing servers, and specialized equipment must adhere strictly to the authorization boundaries defined in OPS-002. Direct consumer purchases without proper approval are strictly ineligible for capital onboarding.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
All hardware procurement transactions and corporate capital acquisitions must conform to the authorization tiers established under OPS-002. In addition, this governance standard receives incoming asset lifecycle references from workstation provisioning workflows in ITS-001 and software asset tracking in ITS-004.
