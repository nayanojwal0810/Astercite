# Asset Procurement and Ownership

## Purpose and Scope
This policy defines enterprise ownership rules, custodial responsibilities, lifecycle tracking, and return protocols for physical computing equipment, specialized lab hardware, and office assets at Aster Systems. It applies to staff members and operational managers across the company. Systematic asset management ensures equipment accountability, inventory accuracy, and proper asset preservation.

## Asset Register Ownership
Centralized custodial tracking ensures hardware accountability, software license alignment, and rapid audit reconciliation across enterprise sites.
<!-- fact_id: OPS005_F01 -->
The centralized master company asset register is maintained, audited, and formally owned by the IT Support department, which records serial numbers, device specifications, and hardware status updates. Centralized ownership provides complete inventory visibility across company locations.

## Equipment Return
When personnel separate from Aster Systems, assigned physical property must be surrendered to maintain corporate asset control.
<!-- fact_id: OPS005_F02 -->
Departing employees and contractors are required to return all assigned corporate hardware, security badges, and peripherals within 5 business days following their official exit date. Returning equipment promptly enables IT personnel to inspect and reassign corporate property.

## New Equipment Purchases
Hardware acquisitions must follow controlled procurement pathways to prevent redundant stock accumulation and preserve capital resources.
<!-- fact_id: OPS005_F03 -->
Requisitions for newly acquired corporate hardware and capital equipment must adhere to the purchasing approval guidelines defined in OPS-002. Routing procurement requests through approved purchasing channels ensures proper financial oversight.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
Hardware procurement transactions and corporate capital acquisitions connect directly to the company purchase approval process for required spending authorizations. In addition, this governance standard receives incoming asset lifecycle references from workstation provisioning workflows in ITS-001 and software asset tracking in ITS-004.
