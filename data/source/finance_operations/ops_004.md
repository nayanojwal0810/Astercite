# Expense Claim Submission Process

## Purpose and Scope
This document outlines the standard procedure for submitting, validating, and reimbursing business-related expenditures incurred by employees on behalf of Aster Systems. It applies to all permanent staff, managers, and operational teams claiming out-of-pocket operational costs.

## Expense Claim Deadline
Timely submission of business expenses ensures prompt financial reconciliation, accurate accounting records, and predictable reimbursement cycles.
<!-- fact_id: OPS004_F01 -->
All out-of-pocket corporate expenditures must be submitted through the enterprise expense portal within 30 calendar_days from the date the cost was incurred. Claims submitted after this filing period may be denied reimbursement absent executive approval.

## Receipt Requirements
Legitimate substantiation is legally required to validate corporate expense deductions and prevent tax reporting non-compliance.
<!-- fact_id: OPS004_F02 -->
Employees must attach original itemized tax invoices or digital receipts for any single purchase item or incurred expense equal to or exceeding 500 inr. Items below this threshold may be claimed with brief written context on the expense filing.

## Finance Review Timeline
Dedicated accounting staff review submitted claims to ensure arithmetic accuracy, valid receipts, and adherence to company policies.
<!-- fact_id: OPS004_F03 -->
The finance department aims to review, audit, and approve compliant expense submissions within 5 business_days of formal ticket submission. Approved reimbursements are credited in the subsequent standard corporate payroll distribution cycle.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
Expenses that exceed frontline departmental purchasing discretion or require elevated administrative sign-off follow the authorization rules specified in OPS-002. Furthermore, this claim submission framework receives incoming reimbursement filings from travel activities under OPS-001 and professional training claims originating from HR-004.
