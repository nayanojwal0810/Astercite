# Expense Claim Submission Process

## Purpose and Scope
This document outlines the standard procedure for submitting, validating, and reimbursing business-related expenditures incurred by employees on behalf of Aster Systems. It applies to regular personnel and managers claiming operational out-of-pocket expenses. Standardized expense processing ensures prompt financial reconciliation, accurate accounting records, and predictable employee reimbursements.

## Expense Claim Deadline
Timely submission of business expenses ensures financial records remain current and simplifies monthly expenditure reconciliation.
<!-- fact_id: OPS004_F01 -->
All out-of-pocket corporate expenditures must be submitted through the enterprise expense portal within 30 calendar days from the date the cost was incurred. Filing claims promptly allows finance staff to review documentation and reconcile departmental budgets efficiently.

## Receipt Requirements
Legitimate documentation verifies that submitted expenditures represent valid business activities conducted on behalf of the organization.
<!-- fact_id: OPS004_F02 -->
Receipts are required for claims above INR 500 to substantiate incurred expenditures and support financial auditing standards. Providing itemized receipts for claims exceeding this threshold confirms transaction details and ensures corporate compliance.

## Finance Review Timeline
Dedicated accounting personnel review submitted claims to verify arithmetic accuracy, receipts, and adherence to company policies.
<!-- fact_id: OPS004_F03 -->
The finance department aims to review, audit, and process compliant expense submissions within 5 business days of formal submission. Consistent review timelines ensure employees receive timely processing of approved reimbursement filings.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
Expenses involving broader procurement activities or elevated purchasing commitments connect to separate purchase approval procedures. Furthermore, this claim submission framework receives incoming reimbursement filings from business travel activities under OPS-001 and professional training claims originating from HR-004.

## Revision History
Version v1 is effective from 2026-11-01. This initial procedure establishes standard expense submission windows and receipt verification thresholds.
