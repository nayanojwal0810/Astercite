# Vendor Onboarding Checklist

## Purpose and Scope
This document sets forth verification criteria, onboarding timelines, and approval requirements for registering third-party suppliers, service contractors, and hardware vendors at Aster Systems. It governs commercial partnerships managed by team leads and finance operations personnel. Systematic onboarding ensures commercial compliance and proper accounting setup prior to transaction execution.

## Vendor Onboarding Timeline
Establishing efficient supplier relationships requires timely review of submitted vendor records and registration in accounting directories.
<!-- fact_id: OPS003_F01 -->
Under standard operating conditions, the finance operations department aims to complete vendor profile validation and onboarding within 5 business days following receipt of all mandatory information. Completing registration within this timeframe enables teams to initiate commercial partnerships smoothly.

## Required Vendor Documents
Verifying necessary administrative and banking details ensures accurate tax reporting and enables secure electronic disbursements.
<!-- fact_id: OPS003_F02 -->
Prior to commercial system activation, prospective vendors must submit valid tax registration and verified bank details to support statutory tax compliance and electronic payment processing. Accurate records prevent payment routing errors and facilitate smooth invoicing cycles.

## First Purchase Approval
Initial commercial transactions with newly registered vendors warrant verification to confirm compliance with corporate procurement standards.
<!-- fact_id: OPS003_F03 -->
Before an initial purchase order or disbursement can be issued to a newly onboarded vendor, explicit approval must be granted by Finance staff. Financial verification confirms that documentation requirements have concluded satisfactorily before funds are committed.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
This checklist and vetting procedure operates independently as a dedicated compliance control and maintains no external document dependencies. Vendor evaluations follow standard accounting verification protocols.

Version v1 is effective from 2026-11-01. This initial checklist establishes standard vendor onboarding timelines and accounting compliance requirements.
