# Vendor Onboarding Checklist

## Purpose and Scope
This document sets forth mandatory due diligence criteria, validation checklists, and compliance requirements for onboarding third-party suppliers, service contractors, and hardware vendors at Aster Systems. It governs all procurement partnerships managed by department leads and finance operations teams.

## Vendor Onboarding Timeline
Establishing efficient commercial relationships requires timely completion of compliance checks, legal reviews, and accounting system registrations.
<!-- fact_id: OPS003_F01 -->
Under standard operating conditions, the finance operations department aims to complete vendor profile validation and directory enrollment within 5 business_days following receipt of all mandatory intake documentation.

## Required Vendor Documents
Comprehensive legal and financial documentation verifies legitimate corporate standing and mitigates risk of fraudulent transactions.
<!-- fact_id: OPS003_F02 -->
Prior to commercial system activation, prospective vendors must submit authenticated tax_registration_and_verified_bank_details alongside company registration certificates to enable statutory tax compliance and secure electronic disbursements.

## First Purchase Approval
Initial commercial transactions with newly registered vendors warrant rigorous verification to confirm operational scope and pricing alignment.
<!-- fact_id: OPS003_F03 -->
Before any initial purchase order or financial disbursement can be issued to a newly registered vendor, explicit written authorization must be granted by finance_staff to confirm that all compliance verifications have concluded satisfactorily.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
This checklist and vetting procedure operates independently as a dedicated compliance control and maintains no external document dependencies. Long-term master services agreements remain subject to legal review outside standard onboarding queues.
