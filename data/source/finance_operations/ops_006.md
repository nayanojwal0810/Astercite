# Business Continuity & Facilities Procedure

## Purpose and Scope
This procedure outlines disaster recovery preparations, facility emergency management, operational resilience standards, and critical incident escalation channels across Aster Systems facilities. It applies to all physical work sites, operational data centers, and corporate personnel.

## Continuity Testing
Periodic disaster simulations and failover exercises validate procedural readiness, test backup communication channels, and ensure system redundancy.
<!-- fact_id: OPS006_F01 -->
Operational disaster recovery drills, secondary workspace activations, and backup infrastructure tests must be performed at a regular frequency of 6 months across all operational hubs. Results and remediation plans are submitted to executive leadership.

## Critical Service Recovery
Rapid operational restoration following unforeseen outages minimizes disruption to client commitments and essential corporate operations.
<!-- fact_id: OPS006_F02 -->
In the event of severe physical facility compromise or infrastructure failure, the mandated maximum target for restoring essential business operations is 4 hours from incident declaration. Critical communication channels receive immediate failover priority.

## Continuity Escalation
Clear command structures ensure decisive decision-making during severe disruptions, physical hazards, or utility blackouts.
<!-- fact_id: OPS006_F03 -->
During active emergency occurrences or sudden site disruptions, personnel must direct operational alerts immediately to the assigned duty_team_lead on call for emergency response coordination. Incident coordinators direct building evacuations and remote failover workflows.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
This emergency management procedure operates independently as the foundational operational resilience guideline across Aster Systems facilities and maintains no external document dependencies. Local building safety codes and civic regulations supersede internal guidelines in the event of conflict.
