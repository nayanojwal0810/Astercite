# Business Continuity & Facilities Procedure

## Purpose and Scope
This procedure outlines disaster recovery preparations, facility emergency management, operational resilience standards, and critical incident escalation channels across Aster Systems facilities. It applies to physical work sites, operational centers, and corporate personnel. Maintaining clear continuity procedures ensures the organization can respond effectively to major operational disruptions.

## Continuity Testing
Periodic disaster simulations and failover exercises validate procedural readiness, test backup communication channels, and ensure operational redundancy.
<!-- fact_id: OPS006_F01 -->
Operational disaster recovery drills, secondary workspace activations, and backup infrastructure tests must be performed at a regular frequency of every 6 months across all operational hubs. Regular testing confirms that recovery plans remain viable and operational teams are prepared for emergencies.

## Critical Service Recovery
Rapid operational restoration following unforeseen outages minimizes disruption to client commitments and essential corporate operations.
<!-- fact_id: OPS006_F02 -->
In the event of severe physical facility compromise or infrastructure failure, the mandated maximum target for restoring essential business operations is 4 hours from incident declaration. Defining an explicit recovery target focuses emergency resources on re-establishing core business functionality.

## Continuity Escalation
Clear command structures ensure decisive communication and structured decision-making during severe disruptions, physical hazards, or facility emergencies.
<!-- fact_id: OPS006_F03 -->
During active emergency occurrences or sudden site disruptions, personnel must direct operational alerts immediately to the assigned duty team lead for emergency response coordination. Escalating to the duty lead ensures rapid assessment and coordinated emergency action.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
This emergency management procedure operates independently as the foundational operational resilience guideline across Aster Systems facilities and maintains no external document dependencies. Building operations follow established safety protocols during active emergencies.
