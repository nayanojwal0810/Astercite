# Deployment & Rollback Procedure

## Purpose and Scope

This procedure outlines the release management framework, approved production deployment schedules, and mandatory rollback criteria for software services at Aster Systems. The objective is to ensure that software updates, patches, and database migrations are delivered safely to production while maintaining operational stability and minimal downtime. This process applies to all software engineers, site reliability engineers, and engineering team leads deploying changes to live customer-facing infrastructure.

## Standard Deployment Days

<!-- fact_id: ENG003_F01 -->

Routine production releases are scheduled exclusively on Tuesday and Thursday. Concentrating routine deployments on these designated midweek days ensures that adequate engineering and operational support resources are actively available to address unexpected regressions or performance degradations without encroaching on weekend monitoring rotations.

Deployments outside of these standard days are restricted and may only proceed under approved emergency change exceptions authorized by engineering leadership.

## Deployment Window

<!-- fact_id: ENG003_F02 -->

All planned production releases must be initiated and executed within the approved deployment window of 14:00-17:00 IST. Conducting releases during this afternoon window aligns with core team availability while avoiding peak customer traffic periods across client business hours.

Any deployment activity that exceeds this window must be halted and either completed through explicit extension authorization or returned to the preceding stable build before the window closes.

## Rollback Conditions

<!-- fact_id: ENG003_F03 -->

Engineers must immediately abort a release and initiate automated rollback procedures if system telemetry indicates that the production error rate is greater than 5% for 10 consecutive minutes following deployment. This threshold represents an unacceptable degradation in service reliability and requires immediate reversal to the previous stable release artifact.

Once the rollback is triggered, the release team must verify traffic recovery across all application instances, confirm database integrity, and inform on-call responders of the deployment reversal.

## Exceptions and Related Documents

Routine deployments must never bypass automated continuous integration validation, artifact signing, or staged verification checks. Emergency deployments required to resolve critical vulnerabilities follow expedited escalation workflows.

All production releases governed by this procedure must comply with change management governance. Specifically, deployments follow change approval requirements defined in ENG-004 before code is promoted to live production environments.
