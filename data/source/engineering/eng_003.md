# Deployment & Rollback Procedure

## Purpose and Scope
This policy governs scheduled software releases, routine deployment intervals, and automated rollback criteria for production microservices and customer-facing infrastructure at Aster Systems. Standardizing release timing and failure recovery thresholds reduces operational disruption and ensures rapid mitigation of unanticipated application regressions. It applies to software engineers, site reliability specialists, and technical leads responsible for service delivery.

## Standard Deployment Days
Restricting production releases to designated days allows engineering teams to coordinate monitoring resources and ensure adequate technical support during rollouts.
<!-- fact_id: ENG003_F01 -->
Normal production deployments are conducted exclusively on Tuesday and Thursday. Scheduling releases on these approved mid-week days ensures engineering teams are available to monitor application health and address any emerging operational issues.

## Deployment Window
Confining releases to defined daytime hours ensures appropriate team overlap and operational support during deployment activities.
<!-- fact_id: ENG003_F02 -->
All standard production releases must take place within the designated deployment window of 14:00 to 17:00 IST. Conducting deployments during these specified hours ensures immediate engineering availability while avoiding high-traffic customer access spikes.

## Rollback Conditions
Continuous post-deployment monitoring enables rapid identification of functional regressions, performance anomalies, and unexpected application errors.
<!-- fact_id: ENG003_F03 -->
A deployment rollback is triggered when telemetry records an error rate greater than 5% for 10 consecutive minutes following rollout. Reverting the release when this threshold is exceeded protects customer workflows and restores baseline service availability.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
All production releases governed by this procedure connect to change management requirements. Specifically, scheduled deployments connect directly to the change approval requirements established in ENG-004.
