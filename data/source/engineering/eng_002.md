# On-call Handover Guide

## Purpose and Scope
This guide defines the operational process for transferring on-call primary rotation duties between outgoing and incoming engineers at Aster Systems. A consistent, structured handover ensures uninterrupted service monitoring, maintains technical context for ongoing system issues, and prevents unaddressed anomalies from slipping through rotation transitions. This procedure applies to engineers participating in production on-call rotations across the engineering department.

## Handover Schedule
Regular rotation scheduling provides predictable coverage transitions and ensures incoming responders are prepared to assume operational responsibilities.
<!-- fact_id: ENG002_F01 -->
On-call rotation transitions occur weekly on Friday, ensuring that the incoming engineer is fully briefed before assuming primary operational duties. Clear scheduling maintains continuous coverage across production services.

## Handover Time
Conducting handover discussions at a consistent operational hour ensures both outgoing and incoming engineers can review recent telemetry and system status together.
<!-- fact_id: ENG002_F02 -->
The standard handover meeting takes place at 18:00 IST, allowing sufficient time for a structured review of active alerts, ongoing maintenance, and recent configuration changes across production environments.

## Required Handover Information
Thorough documentation of current operational context ensures smooth continuity and prevents miscommunication between shifts.
<!-- fact_id: ENG002_F03 -->
During the transition session, the outgoing engineer must provide complete status details covering open incidents, assigned owners, and next actions for all unresolved operational items. Documenting these items ensures the incoming responder has complete visibility into ongoing remediation efforts.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
This handover process receives incoming incident follow-up items from production incident response procedures under ENG-001. When ongoing incidents or unresolved production issues require escalation beyond the shift responder, engineers refer to service ownership information maintained in the restricted service directory to engage the appropriate service owners.

Version v1 is effective from 2026-11-01. This initial guide standardizes the weekly on-call transition schedule, time window, and required operational documentation for Aster Systems engineering rotations.
