# On-call Handover Guide

## Purpose and Scope

This guide defines the formal operational process for transferring on-call primary rotation duties between outgoing and incoming engineers at Aster Systems. A consistent, structured handover ensures uninterrupted service monitoring, maintains technical context for ongoing system issues, and prevents unaddressed anomalies from slipping through rotation transitions. This procedure applies to all engineers participating in primary or secondary production on-call rotations across the engineering department.

## Handover Schedule

<!-- fact_id: ENG002_F01 -->

Rotation transitions occur weekly on Friday, ensuring that the incoming engineer is fully briefed and in command before the start of the weekend operational window. Both the outgoing and incoming engineers must coordinate directly to complete the shift transition without interruption to active monitoring coverage.

If the scheduled rotation day coincides with a declared public holiday, the engineers must complete the sync session on the preceding business day while keeping shift alarms active until the standard rotation cutoff.

## Handover Time

<!-- fact_id: ENG002_F02 -->

The mandatory handover meeting takes place at 18:00 IST, allowing sufficient time for a comprehensive review of weekly operational metrics, unacknowledged alerts, and active infrastructure tasks. Both parties must confirm their attendance and conduct the briefing synchronously via video call or designated on-call collaboration channels.

Primary paging responsibilities and automated rotation routes officially transition in the alerting system immediately upon completion of this 18:00 IST briefing.

## Required Handover Information

<!-- fact_id: ENG002_F03 -->

During the transition session, the outgoing engineer must review and document all active system states, explicitly recording open incidents, the current designated owner, and the agreed next action for each ongoing item. Incomplete or ambiguous action items must not be transferred without an assigned engineer accountable for investigation.

The handover log must also note any recent configuration changes, ongoing maintenance windows, flaky alert thresholds, or transient infrastructure anomalies observed during the preceding shift.

## Exceptions and Related Documents

Shift swaps or emergency handover postponements require prior review and written approval from the engineering team lead. The outgoing engineer remains on active duty until the replacement responder formally accepts rotation ownership.

This guide receives incoming incident follow-up items from production incident response procedures governed by ENG-001. When ongoing incidents or unresolved production issues require escalation beyond the shift responder, engineers follow the escalation guidance in ENG-006 to engage appropriate service owners.
