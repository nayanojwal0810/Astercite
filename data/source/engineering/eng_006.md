# Service Ownership & Escalation Directory

## Purpose and Scope

This directory establishes authoritative ownership boundaries and escalation routing for core architectural services operated by Aster Systems. Precise ownership mapping ensures that critical architectural decisions, security escalations, and incident routing are directed to the correct specialized teams without ambiguous handoffs. Due to the sensitive operational context and leadership escalation mandates contained herein, this directory is restricted exclusively to authorized engineering team leads.

## Portal Service Ownership

<!-- fact_id: ENG006_F01 -->

The customer portal and web interface service (svc-portal) is owned and operated by the Platform Team. The Platform Team maintains primary responsibility for frontend gateway services, web application delivery networks, user interface asset delivery, and customer-facing dashboard infrastructure.

Technical escalations regarding portal availability, user interface latency, or client integration failures must be routed directly to the Platform Team lead for triage.

## Authentication Service Ownership

<!-- fact_id: ENG006_F02 -->

The centralized authentication and authorization service (svc-auth) is owned and operated by the Identity Team. The Identity Team manages user credential stores, single sign-on federation, cryptographic session signing, API token lifecycles, and access policy evaluation.

Security anomalies, token generation failures, or authentication gateway degradations require immediate escalation to the Identity Team lead.

## Billing Service Ownership

<!-- fact_id: ENG006_F03 -->

The enterprise billing, invoicing, and subscription accounting service (svc-billing) is owned and operated by the Payments Team. The Payments Team oversees recurring charge processing, third-party gateway integrations, invoice calculation engines, and financial reconciliation pipelines.

Operational discrepancies involving failed billing synchronization, gateway timeouts, or ledger reconciliation exceptions must be escalated to the Payments Team lead.

## Exceptions and Related Documents

Temporary service reassignments or joint-ownership arrangements across microservices require written authorization from engineering executive leadership.

This directory receives incoming service escalation inquiries from on-call handovers and incident management procedures governed by ENG-002. It maintains no outgoing cross-document dependencies or secondary workflow instructions.

## Revision History

Version v1 is effective from 2026-11-01. This initial restricted directory establishes foundational team ownership assignments for svc-portal, svc-auth, and svc-billing across Aster Systems infrastructure.
