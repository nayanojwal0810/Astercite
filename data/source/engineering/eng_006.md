# Service Ownership & Escalation Directory

## Purpose and Scope
This directory establishes authoritative ownership boundaries and escalation routing for core architectural services operated by Aster Systems. Precise ownership mapping ensures that architectural decisions, operational escalations, and incident inquiries are directed to the appropriate specialized teams. Due to the sensitive operational context contained herein, this directory is restricted exclusively to authorized engineering team leads.

## Portal Service Ownership
Frontend application gateways and customer-facing interfaces require dedicated operational oversight and ongoing architectural maintenance.
<!-- fact_id: ENG006_F01 -->
The customer portal and web interface service (svc-portal) is owned and operated by the Platform Team. The Platform Team maintains responsibility for frontend delivery networks, user interface assets, and customer-facing dashboard infrastructure.

## Authentication Service Ownership
Identity infrastructure, directory federation, and secure token issuance require specialized security governance and operational controls.
<!-- fact_id: ENG006_F02 -->
The centralized authentication and authorization service (svc-auth) is owned and operated by the Identity Team. The Identity Team manages user credential stores, single sign-on federation, and cryptographic session validation.

## Billing Service Ownership
Subscription management, payment processing, and accounting integrations demand rigorous financial accuracy and technical monitoring.
<!-- fact_id: ENG006_F03 -->
The enterprise billing, invoicing, and subscription accounting service (svc-billing) is owned and operated by the Payments Team. The Payments Team oversees charge processing, gateway integrations, invoice calculation, and financial reconciliation pipelines.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
This directory receives incoming service escalation inquiries from on-call handovers and incident management procedures governed by ENG-002. It maintains no outgoing cross-document dependencies or secondary workflow instructions.

## Revision History
Version v1 is effective from 2026-11-01. This initial restricted directory establishes foundational team ownership assignments for svc-portal, svc-auth, and svc-billing across Aster Systems infrastructure.
