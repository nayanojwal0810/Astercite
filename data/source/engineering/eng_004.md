# Change Approval Checklist

## Purpose and Scope
This checklist establishes governance standards, risk classifications, approval protocols, and stabilization windows for infrastructure and application modifications across Aster Systems production environments. Controlled change processes ensure system reliability, minimize service disruptions, and maintain full traceability for operational adjustments. It applies to engineers, systems administrators, and engineering team leads planning or implementing technical changes.

## High-Risk Change Approval
Modifications that affect core architectural backbones, customer data schemas, or shared network configurations carry significant risk of service disruption.
<!-- fact_id: ENG004_F01 -->
Any modification categorized as a high-risk change requires formal review and joint authorization from both a team lead and the service owner before implementation can begin. Dual review ensures comprehensive evaluation of potential operational impact and rollback readiness.

## Migration Change Freeze
Major architectural migrations and infrastructure upgrades require stable environments free from concurrent non-essential adjustments.
<!-- fact_id: ENG004_F02 -->
A mandatory change freeze period of 24 hours is enforced prior to and during planned migration events, prohibiting unrelated production modifications during the freeze window. Maintaining environment stability during migrations protects data integrity and reduces troubleshooting complexity.

## Emergency Change Approval
When immediate emergency remediation is necessary to restore degraded production services, rapid action takes precedence over standard advance reviews.
<!-- fact_id: ENG004_F03 -->
Following the execution of an emergency change, engineers must submit documentation and obtain retrospective approval within 1 business day of change completion. Retrospective review ensures that emergency actions are properly evaluated, documented, and integrated into long-term configuration records.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
This checklist receives incoming change approval requests for scheduled production deployments governed by ENG-003. It maintains no outgoing cross-document dependencies or secondary workflow instructions.

## Revision History
Version v1 is effective from 2026-11-01. This initial checklist standardizes approval matrices for high-risk modifications, establishes migration freeze periods, and sets retrospective review deadlines for Aster Systems engineering changes.
