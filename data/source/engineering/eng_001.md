# Production Incident Severity & Escalation Policy

## Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes the operational criteria for classifying, acknowledging, and escalating production incidents across Aster Systems customer-facing platforms and enterprise services. Its primary objective is to minimize customer impact through rapid triage, structured technical communication, and predictable remediation workflows. The procedures outlined herein apply directly to all on-call engineers, secondary responders, and engineering team leads responsible for production infrastructure and core application availability.

## P1 Incident Acknowledgment

<!-- fact_id: ENG001_F01 -->
Critical production outages designated as Priority 1 (P1) represent catastrophic service degradations affecting customer production environments. Designated on-call engineers must acknowledge all P1 incident alerts within 10 minutes of automated notification or manual escalation dispatch. Immediate acknowledgment triggers a dedicated incident bridge and alerts engineering leadership.

If the primary responder fails to confirm receipt within this window, the alerting system escalates to the secondary on-call engineer and the engineering team lead. The responder who claims the incident assumes operational command.

## P2 Incident Acknowledgment

<!-- fact_id: ENG001_F02 -->
Major production incidents designated as Priority 2 (P2) indicate substantial service impairments or degradations where core workflows remain partially operational. On-call engineers are required to acknowledge P2 incident alerts within 30 minutes of receipt.

Upon acknowledgment, the responding engineer evaluates telemetry dashboards to identify the root cause or formulate a containment plan, providing regular status updates until the degradation is remediated.

## P1 Postmortem Requirements

<!-- fact_id: ENG001_F03 -->
Following the mitigation and resolution of any P1 incident, the responsible engineering team must conduct a blameless postmortem review and publish a complete incident report within 2 business days. The postmortem document must record the root cause, timeline, customer impact, and preventative action items.

## Exceptions and Related Documents

This policy governs all active production systems maintained by Aster Systems. Temporary deviations from standard acknowledgment timelines require prior authorization from the engineering team lead.

Active incident remediation workflows and outstanding post-incident action items connect with regular operational handover procedures. Specifically, incident follow-up tasks and open monitoring requirements are transitioned to incoming on-call personnel during scheduled handover sessions in accordance with ENG-002.
