# Production Incident Severity & Escalation Policy

## Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes operational criteria for classifying, acknowledging, and responding to production incidents across Aster Systems customer-facing platforms and enterprise services. Its primary objective is to minimize customer impact through rapid triage, clear communication, and predictable remediation workflows. The procedures apply to on-call engineers and engineering team leads responsible for production infrastructure and core application availability.

## P1 Incident Acknowledgment
Critical production outages designated as Priority 1 (P1) represent catastrophic service degradations or complete loss of core application functionality affecting customer production environments. Prompt acknowledgment ensures rapid response and timely containment of critical failures.
<!-- fact_id: ENG001_F01 -->
Designated on-call engineers must acknowledge all P1 incident alerts within 10 minutes of automated notification or escalation dispatch. Acknowledging the alert confirms that an active responder has begun technical investigation and initial diagnostic assessment.

## P2 Incident Acknowledgment
Major production incidents designated as Priority 2 (P2) indicate substantial service impairments, performance degradations, or failure of non-critical system components where core business workflows remain partially operational. Structured response procedures allow engineering teams to address significant anomalies efficiently.
<!-- fact_id: ENG001_F02 -->
On-call engineers are required to acknowledge P2 incident alerts within 30 minutes of receipt. Timely initial acknowledgment allows engineers to evaluate telemetry dashboards, assess error trends, and formulate appropriate containment measures.

## P1 Postmortem Requirements
Thorough post-incident analysis helps engineering teams understand failure modes, prevent recurrence, and improve overall system resilience across services.
<!-- fact_id: ENG001_F03 -->
Following the mitigation and resolution of any P1 incident, the responsible engineering team must conduct a blameless postmortem review and publish a complete incident report within 2 business days. The postmortem document records the root cause, timeline of events, customer impact, and preventative action items.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
This policy governs production systems maintained by Aster Systems. Active incident remediation workflows and outstanding post-incident follow-up connect with regular operational handover procedures. Specifically, incident follow-up tasks and open monitoring requirements connect directly to on-call handover sessions governed by ENG-002.
