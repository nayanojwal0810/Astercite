# Laptop Provisioning and Replacement

## Purpose and Scope

This policy outlines the fulfillment timelines, deployment standards, and replacement workflows for employee computing hardware at Aster Systems. Standardized provisioning ensures that all staff receive secure, fully configured workstations capable of supporting their job responsibilities while maintaining corporate endpoint security benchmarks. This document applies to all company personnel requesting hardware, as well as IT Support specialists tasked with device preparation and lifecycle maintenance.

## Standard Laptop Provisioning

<!-- fact_id: ITS001_F01 -->

IT Support operates under a standard laptop provisioning target of 3 business days from the receipt of an approved hardware request or confirmed onboarding notification. During this 3-day window, IT specialists image the machine, configure baseline endpoint security software, register corporate certificates, and verify hardware diagnostics before issuing the device.

Hiring managers and department coordinators must plan equipment lead times accordingly to guarantee workstation availability on or before a new team member's first day of work.

## Urgent Laptop Replacement

<!-- fact_id: ITS001_F02 -->

When an assigned workstation suffers catastrophic hardware failure, unrecoverable system corruption, or verified security compromise, IT Support enforces an urgent security replacement target of 1 business day. Expedited replacement minimizes productive downtime for affected employees and ensures immediate containment of potential hardware vulnerabilities.

To initiate an urgent replacement, the affected employee or their lead must file an urgent service ticket with IT Support documenting the operational impact and device fault symptoms.

## Equipment Purchase Policies

<!-- fact_id: ITS001_F03 -->

Procurement of non-standard laptop configurations, specialized developer workstations, or inventory replenishment hardware is governed by corporate purchasing guidelines referenced in OPS-005 and OPS-002. These external governance frameworks define asset ownership lifecycle requirements and formal expenditure approval authorities.

IT Support verifies that all hardware acquisitions adhere to these referenced operational specifications before placing vendor purchase orders.

## Exceptions and Related Documents

Temporary loaner laptops may be issued from reserved local inventory when custom hardware configurations face supplier delays, subject to availability.

This policy maintains directed operational dependencies with related corporate processes. Specifically, laptop replacement follows asset ownership rules defined in OPS-005. Separately, this policy receives incoming laptop fulfillment requests connected with account provisioning workflows under ITS-002.
