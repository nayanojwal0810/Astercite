# Laptop Provisioning and Replacement

## Purpose and Scope
This policy outlines fulfillment timelines, deployment standards, and replacement workflows for employee computing hardware at Aster Systems. Standardized provisioning ensures that staff receive secure, properly configured workstations supporting their job responsibilities while maintaining corporate endpoint security benchmarks. This document applies to company personnel requesting hardware and IT Support specialists responsible for device preparation.

## Standard Laptop Provisioning
Predictable hardware preparation timelines allow departments to plan equipment onboarding and ensure new personnel can begin work smoothly.
<!-- fact_id: ITS001_F01 -->
IT Support operates under a standard laptop provisioning target of 3 business days from receipt of an approved hardware request. During this 3-day window, IT specialists image the machine, configure baseline endpoint security software, and verify hardware readiness before issuing the device.

## Urgent Laptop Replacement
Security incidents affecting assigned hardware require expedited containment and prompt workstation replacement to maintain data protection.
<!-- fact_id: ITS001_F02 -->
When an assigned workstation is affected by a verified security compromise, IT Support enforces an urgent security replacement target of 1 business day. Expedited replacement ensures immediate containment of potential endpoint security risks while minimizing operational downtime.

## Equipment Purchase Policies
Non-standard hardware configurations and equipment purchasing require alignment with corporate asset management and expenditure frameworks.
<!-- fact_id: ITS001_F03 -->
Procurement of non-standard laptop configurations and replenishment hardware is governed by corporate purchasing guidelines referenced in OPS-005 and OPS-002. These external frameworks define asset lifecycle requirements and formal expenditure approval authorities.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
This policy maintains directed operational connections with related corporate processes. Specifically, laptop replacement follows asset ownership rules defined in OPS-005. Separately, this policy receives incoming workstation fulfillment requests connected with employee account provisioning workflows.

## Revision History
Version v1 is effective from 2026-11-01. This initial baseline formalizes standard and urgent hardware provisioning turnaround times and establishes procurement policy alignment for Aster Systems workstations.
