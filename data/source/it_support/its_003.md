# MFA and Password Reset Procedure

## Purpose and Scope

This procedure establishes mandatory protocols for verifying user identities and re-establishing multi-factor authentication (MFA) devices or account passwords at Aster Systems. Standardized authentication recovery processes prevent social engineering exploits, credential interception, and unauthorized account takeover while ensuring that legitimate employees regain system access promptly. This document applies to all company employees, contractors, and IT Support helpdesk technicians handling identity recovery requests.

## MFA Reset Verification

<!-- fact_id: ITS003_F01 -->

IT Support specialists must require both positive identity verification and an active service ticket before initiating any MFA device reset or seed token regeneration. Identity verification requires a live video verification with visual corporate ID confirmation or manager-attested voice verification through authenticated internal phone extensions.

Technicians must independently verify the employee record in the human resources management system and log the confirmation details directly into the corresponding service ticket prior to clearing existing MFA bindings.

## MFA Reset Resolution

<!-- fact_id: ITS003_F02 -->

Following successful identity confirmation, IT Support operates under a standard MFA reset resolution target of 4 business hours. This turnaround window ensures that locked-out personnel can resume their daily business activities without prolonged operational delay.

During this 4-hour window, the technician resets the authentication factor, generates a temporary one-time enrollment token, and guides the employee through re-registering their designated mobile authenticator application.

## Prohibited Reset Practices

<!-- fact_id: ITS003_F03 -->

Delivering temporary MFA enrollment tokens, bypass codes, or password reset credentials by email is strictly prohibited under all circumstances. Because unauthenticated account lockouts frequently coincide with compromised email accounts or forwarded inboxes, transmitting recovery secrets via email creates an unacceptable security exposure.

All temporary enrollment codes must be communicated verbally over an authenticated video session or displayed via a single-use secure internal portal accessible only during active identity verification.

## Exceptions and Related Documents

Executive and emergency field resets outside normal support coverage hours follow designated on-call identity verification procedures authorized by the IT support manager.

This document serves as an independent technical security standard. It maintains no external cross-document dependencies, incoming workflows, or outbound policy links.

Version v1 is effective from 2026-11-01. This initial procedure establishes dual-factor verification requirements, a 4-business-hour SLA, and strict prohibitions on email credential delivery for Aster Systems accounts.
