# IT Support Ticket Priorities and SLAs

## Purpose and Scope
This standard establishes priority classifications and initial response time targets for technical requests submitted to the IT Support service desk. Clear priority definitions help support engineers triage incoming inquiries effectively and provide predictable response expectations for employees across all departments at Aster Systems.

## P1 Ticket Response
Priority 1 (Critical) incidents represent severe disruptions impacting entire departments, critical production networks, or mission-essential infrastructure.
<!-- fact_id: ITS006_F01 -->
For incidents designated as P1 Critical, IT Support engineers must acknowledge and initiate active technical troubleshooting within 15 minutes of initial ticket creation. Rapid response ensures immediate focus on critical disruptions affecting broad operational capabilities.

## P2 Ticket Response
Priority 2 (High) tickets reflect significant operational impediments affecting team productivity or core business functions without complete site paralysis.
<!-- fact_id: ITS006_F02 -->
Support engineers are required to provide substantive initial response and diagnostics within 2 business hours for all registered P2 High priority tickets. Prioritizing high-impact issues ensures key business operations can resume promptly.

## P3 Ticket Response
Priority 3 (Standard) requests encompass routine workstation inquiries, minor functional defects, user preference adjustments, and non-urgent service requests.
<!-- fact_id: ITS006_F03 -->
The agreed service desk response target for addressing P3 Standard requests is 1 business day following submission during regular operational hours. Standard requests are addressed systematically to maintain steady workflow progression.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
This service level specification operates independently across general support interactions and maintains no external document dependencies. Service triage follows established priority classifications across all operational units.

## Revision History
Version v1 is effective from 2026-11-01. This initial specification formalizes service desk priority classifications and baseline response time commitments.
