# IT Support Ticket Priorities and SLAs

## Purpose and Scope
This standard establishes priority classifications, initial response time targets, and resolution service level agreements (SLAs) for technical requests submitted to the IT support service desk. It governs expectations across all operational units within Aster Systems.

## P1 Ticket Response
Priority 1 (Critical) incidents represent severe disruptions impacting entire departments, critical production networks, or mission-essential infrastructure.
<!-- fact_id: ITS006_F01 -->
For incidents designated as P1 Critical, IT support engineers must acknowledge and initiate active technical troubleshooting within 15 minutes of initial ticket creation. A dedicated incident manager coordinates continuous technical triage until system functionality is restored.

## P2 Ticket Response
Priority 2 (High) tickets reflect significant operational impediments affecting team productivity or core business functions without complete site paralysis.
<!-- fact_id: ITS006_F02 -->
Support engineers are required to provide substantive initial response and diagnostics within 2 business_hours for all registered P2 High priority tickets. Technicians prioritize remediation work ahead of non-blocking inquiries.

## P3 Ticket Response
Priority 3 (Standard) requests encompass standard workstation issues, minor functional defects, user preference adjustments, and non-urgent service requests.
<!-- fact_id: ITS006_F03 -->
The agreed service desk response target for addressing P3 Standard requests is 1 business_days following submission during regular operational hours. Routine tickets are queued according to timestamp order.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
This service level specification operates independently across all general support interactions and maintains no external document dependencies. Departmental escalation paths remain subject to internal service desk operational guidelines.
