# Device Loss or Theft Response

## Purpose and Scope
This document defines emergency response protocols for lost, stolen, or compromised company-issued hardware, including laptops, mobile phones, and security authentication tokens. This operational policy applies across Aster Systems to protect internal data and prevent unauthorized system intrusion.

## Lost Device Reporting
Immediate notification is critical to preventing malicious access to corporate resources when a hardware asset goes missing.
<!-- fact_id: ITS005_F01 -->
Any employee who discovers that an assigned company device has been lost or stolen must report the occurrence within 1 hours to the IT support emergency response desk. Prompt notification enables security staff to initiate perimeter access defenses.

## Device Access Protection
Upon receiving and authenticating an initial loss notification, support technicians execute immediate defensive procedures through centralized endpoint management.
<!-- fact_id: ITS005_F02 -->
Following incident verification, the mandatory technical action taken by IT support personnel is to disable_device_access across all enterprise networks, identity providers, and local session caches. Remote wiping routines are dispatched where remote connectivity permits.

## Required Incident Records
Detailed documentation of all hardware losses ensures auditability, insurance tracking, and forensic investigation.
<!-- fact_id: ITS005_F03 -->
Each reported hardware loss or security theft event mandates the creation and maintenance of a formal it_support_ticket detailing serial numbers, last known coordinates, reported timelines, and containment actions taken.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
This procedure operates independently as a dedicated incident response standard and maintains no external document dependencies. Hardware replacement and inventory reconciliations follow subsequent technical workflows once containment concludes.
