# Device Loss or Theft Response

## Purpose and Scope
This document defines emergency response protocols for lost, stolen, or compromised company-issued hardware, including laptops, mobile devices, and security authentication tokens. This operational policy applies across Aster Systems to protect internal data and prevent unauthorized system intrusion. Standardized containment procedures ensure rapid risk mitigation when physical assets are compromised.

## Lost Device Reporting
Immediate notification is critical to preventing unauthorized access to corporate resources when a hardware asset goes missing.
<!-- fact_id: ITS005_F01 -->
Any employee who discovers that an assigned company device has been lost or stolen must report the occurrence within 1 hour to IT Support. Prompt notification enables support personnel to initiate protective countermeasures without delay.

## Device Access Protection
Upon receiving and authenticating an initial loss notification, support technicians execute immediate defensive procedures through centralized endpoint management.
<!-- fact_id: ITS005_F02 -->
Following incident verification, the mandatory technical action taken by IT Support personnel is to disable access to the device across enterprise networks and authentication systems. Disabling device access prevents unauthorized individuals from reaching sensitive internal resources.

## Required Incident Records
Detailed documentation of hardware losses ensures auditability and supports incident tracking across the organization.
<!-- fact_id: ITS005_F03 -->
Each reported hardware loss or theft event mandates the creation and maintenance of an IT Support ticket recording the incident details and containment actions taken. Formal incident records ensure complete traceability for administrative review.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
This procedure operates independently as a dedicated incident response standard and maintains no external document dependencies. Hardware replacement follows subsequent technical workflows once initial containment concludes.

## Revision History
Version v1 is effective from 2026-11-01. This initial procedure establishes standard protocols for lost or stolen device containment and reporting.
