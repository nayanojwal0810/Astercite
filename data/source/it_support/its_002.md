# Account Provisioning / Deprovisioning

## Purpose and Scope
This restricted procedure establishes operational requirements and turnaround windows for creating, modifying, and deactivating user identity accounts across Aster Systems enterprise systems. Strict control over identity lifecycles safeguards proprietary data and enforces least-privilege access during onboarding and offboarding. Access to this document is restricted exclusively to authorized IT Support and team leads responsible for identity administration.

## New Account Requests
Advance notice enables technical teams to establish directory credentials and configure appropriate group memberships before an employee begins active duty.
<!-- fact_id: ITS002_F01 -->
IT Support requires a standard new account request lead time of 2 business days prior to the required activation date. This lead time provides sufficient opportunity to generate directory credentials, configure access profiles, and assign role-based email groups.

## Employee Exit and Access Deactivation
Prompt account deactivation upon employee separation prevents unauthorized post-employment access and protects enterprise data assets.
<!-- fact_id: ITS002_F02 -->
Following confirmed employee exit, IT Support must execute complete access deactivation across all corporate systems within 4 hours. Timely deactivation terminates active user sessions and revokes enterprise network authentication tokens.

## Account Request Approval
Formal verification of identity requests ensures that user accounts are created only for authorized personnel with validated business needs.
<!-- fact_id: ITS002_F03 -->
Every request for new account provisioning requires explicit approval from HR staff before IT Support initiates credential generation. Verifying authorization with human resources ensures account creation aligns with official personnel records.

## Exceptions and Related Documents
This procedure coordinates closely with related infrastructure processes. Onboarding account provisioning connects directly to hardware fulfillment workflows under ITS-001 to ensure computing assets are prepared in coordination with identity setup.

## Revision History
Version v1 is effective from 2026-11-01. This initial restricted standard sets lead times for new account creation, defines the 4-hour exit deactivation window, and designates HR staff as the approval authority.
