# Account Provisioning / Deprovisioning

## Purpose and Scope

This restricted procedure establishes operational requirements and mandatory turnaround windows for creating, modifying, and deactivating user identity accounts across Aster Systems networks and enterprise systems. Strict control over identity lifecycles safeguards proprietary data, enforces least-privilege access, and ensures audit compliance during onboarding and offboarding. Access to this document is restricted exclusively to authorized IT Support and HR staff responsible for identity administration.

## New Account Requests

<!-- fact_id: ITS002_F01 -->

IT Support requires a standard new account request lead time of 2 business days prior to the required activation date. This lead time provides sufficient opportunity to generate directory credentials, configure single sign-on profiles, assign role-based email groups, and establish multi-factor authentication bindings.

Submissions received with less than 2 business days of advance notice cannot guarantee complete credential availability on the employee's first morning of service.

## Employee Exit and Access Deactivation

<!-- fact_id: ITS002_F02 -->

Upon notification of a confirmed employee departure, IT Support must enforce complete system access deactivation within a strict operational target of 4 hours. Prompt deactivation involves revoking directory credentials, invalidating active authentication tokens, suspending single sign-on access, and terminating remote VPN permissions.

This 4-hour window ensures that departing personnel cannot access confidential company repositories or internal communication platforms following their formal departure time.

## Account Request Approval

<!-- fact_id: ITS002_F03 -->

Every request to provision a new corporate identity, modify existing entitlement levels, or grant temporary administrative rights requires formal authorization from designated HR staff. IT Support specialists are strictly prohibited from generating user credentials or elevating access permissions without documented sign-off from People Operations.

HR staff verify employment status, contract validity, and authorized job role classifications before approving identity creation requests in the administration portal.

## Exceptions and Related Documents

Emergency account suspensions required for immediate security containment may be executed by IT Support on-call personnel, followed by retrospective notification to People Operations.

This procedure coordinates closely with related infrastructure processes. Onboarding account work connects directly to laptop provisioning workflows in ITS-001, and receives incoming account creation requests initiated through employee onboarding under HR-003.

## Revision History

Version v1 is effective from 2026-11-01. This initial restricted standard sets binding lead times for new account creation, defines the 4-hour exit deactivation window, and designates HR staff as the mandatory approval authority.
